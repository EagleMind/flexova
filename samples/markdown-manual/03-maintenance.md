# Maintenance

Regular maintenance keeps the pump reliable in the field.

## Service intervals

| Interval         | Task                                   |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Every use        | Check oil level and clean the strainer |
| Every 25 hours   | Change the oil                          |
| Every 100 hours  | Replace the spark plug                 |

## Changing the oil

1. Let the engine cool down.
2. Remove the drain plug with the 13 mm spanner.
3. Refill with 0.6 L of SAE 10W-30 oil.

> [!CAUTION]
> Used oil is hazardous waste. Take it to a collection point.

> Reliability comes from routine, not from repairs.
>
> — Field team handbook
